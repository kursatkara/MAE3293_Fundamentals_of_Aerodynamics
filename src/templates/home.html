Interactive lessons for MAE 3293, Fundamentals of Aerodynamics, at Oklahoma State University. Each lesson pairs the derivations with figures computed live from the exact relations for a calorically perfect gas: change the Mach number, the geometry, or the back pressure and the wave pattern, the pressure distribution, and the forces update at once. The lessons follow Kursat Kara’s lecture notes and J. D. Anderson, Fundamentals of Aerodynamics.

  • Compressible Aerodynamics Calculator

Notes

Using the lessons

Each lesson is a single self-contained web page. The equations, fonts, and figures are built in, so a lesson keeps working with no network connection once it has loaded; save the page in the browser to keep a copy on a laptop or tablet.

The ratio of specific heats, γ, in the top bar of each lesson applies to every figure on that page. The theme and text size follow the setting chosen on the calculator.

Corrections and suggestions are welcome through the issue tracker.
