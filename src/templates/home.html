Interactive lessons and calculators for MAE 3293, Fundamentals of Aerodynamics, at Oklahoma State University. Each lesson pairs the derivations with figures computed live from the exact relations for a calorically perfect gas: change the Mach number, the geometry, or the back pressure and the wave pattern, the pressure distribution, and the forces update at once. The lessons follow Kursat Kara’s lecture notes and J. D. Anderson, Fundamentals of Aerodynamics.
