Reference

Relations used

Total-to-static ratios

\[ \frac{T_0}{T}=1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2 \tag{8.40} \]
\[ \frac{p_0}{p}=\left(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}} \tag{8.42} \]
\[ \frac{\rho_0}{\rho}=\left(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma-1}} \tag{8.43} \]

Each ratio increases monotonically with M, so a known ratio gives the Mach number directly by solving the relation for M.

Area–Mach number relation

\[ \left(\frac{A}{A^*}\right)^2=\frac{1}{M^2}\left[\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\left(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\right)\right]^{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}} \tag{10.32} \]

A/A* has its minimum, 1, at M = 1, so every A/A* > 1 has two solutions: one subsonic and one supersonic. The branch must be chosen from the flow; the calculator finds the chosen root by bisection.
