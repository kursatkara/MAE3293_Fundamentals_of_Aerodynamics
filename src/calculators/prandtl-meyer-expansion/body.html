Relations used

Prandtl–Meyer function

\[ \nu(M)=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}\tan^{-1}\!\sqrt{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma+1}\left(M^2-1\right)}-\tan^{-1}\!\sqrt{M^2-1} \]
\[ \theta=\nu(M_2)-\nu(M_1),\qquad \mu=\sin^{-1}\frac{1}{M} \]

ν rises monotonically from 0 at M = 1 toward νmax = (π/2)(√((γ+1)/(γ−1)) − 1) as M → ∞ (130.45° for γ = 1.4), so a turn larger than νmax − ν(M1) would expand the flow to zero pressure. The calculator inverts ν by bisection.

Across the fan

\[ p_{0,2}=p_{0,1},\qquad T_{0,2}=T_{0,1} \]
\[ \frac{T_2}{T_1}=\frac{1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_1^2}{1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_2^2},\quad \frac{p_2}{p_1}=\left(\frac{T_2}{T_1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}},\quad \frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}=\left(\frac{T_2}{T_1}\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma-1}} \]

The expansion is isentropic, so the total conditions are unchanged and the static ratios follow from the isentropic relations at M1 and M2.
